Laura Bussi

Lecturer · Informatics Institute, University of Amsterdam

I am a researcher in formal methods and artificial intelligence. I work on spatial and spatio-temporal reasoning, model checking, and the verification of learning-based systems, developing logical and algorithmic foundations for trustworthy AI, with an emphasis on tool-supported methods and safety-critical applications such as medical imaging.

Research

Logical foundations for trustworthy AI

From spatial logics on images and polyhedra to verified neural networks: I build theory and tools that make it possible to reason rigorously about large, structured, and learned systems.

i.
Formal methods for reasoning about large structured systems
ii.
Spatial and topological reasoning
iii.
Neuro-symbolic artificial intelligence
iv.
Trustworthy and explainable machine learning
